# Glossary

**Correction**: the set of changes AutoColor makes to one photo (exposure, white balance, shadows, highlights, black and white points, contrast, vibrance and noise reduction).

**Target band**: the range a measurement of a good photo falls in. A setting changes only when its measurement is outside its band.

**Do no harm**: the rule that a photo whose measurements are all inside their bands is left almost as it was.

**Cast**: a colour tint over the whole photo, such as too warm, too cool or too green, as opposed to a scene that is colourful on purpose.

**Proxy**: a small copy of a photo that measurements are taken on.

**Full path**: correcting a photo in one render at full size. Needs the most memory.

**Banded path**: correcting a large photo strip by strip so memory stays small.

**Luminance error**: the average per pixel difference in lightness (L*) between a photo and its undamaged original.
